# AutoVere – social media pack (English)

Ready to copy. Every number below comes from the live calculator or the route pages (checked 25 Sep 2026).
Toll figures are one-way, car, from the fastest route (high) to the toll-optimised route (low).
Ferries and fuel are extra unless a post says otherwise.

**Rules for all posts**
- Never say "#1", "best in the world" or "the only one". The benchmark score was written by us, and Trip Cost Calculator and TollGuru are real competitors.
- Say "estimate", "range" or "typically". Every number has a range.
- Always end with one link. Route pages: `https://www.autovere.com/routes/<slug>`. Planner: `https://autovere.com`.
- Each route has a ready image: `public/og/routes/<slug>.jpg` (1200×630). Use it as the post image.
- Tag the toll data source when you can: "Toll data: Norwegian Public Roads Administration (NVDB)".

## 4. Longer posts

### LinkedIn – the product story
**Tolls, ferries and fuel are the invisible half of every road trip.**
Maps apps tell you the distance and the time. Very few tell you the cost, and none of them tell you what it will be for your car.
We built AutoVere in Kristiansand to fix that: pick two places, and get one number for tolls, ferries and fuel or charging. It is calculated from the real route and published rates, so the same trip always gives the same answer.
What we learned building it:
1. In Norway, the route matters more than the distance. Oslo → Bergen ranges from NOK 230 to NOK 415 in tolls.
2. Ferries are the hidden cost. They are often larger than the gantry tolls.
3. People trust a range more than a single number, so we show both.
4. Electric cars pay less at most gantries, which changes the maths on cabin roads driven 20+ times a year.
It is free to use, with no account. If you drive in Norway, try your own route: autovere.com

## 5. Reply templates

- **"Is this accurate?"** – It's an estimate built from the national toll register (last checked 13 Sep 2026) and current fuel prices. Real bills can differ a little, for example with an AutoPASS tag (about 20% less). If you tell us what you actually paid, we can improve it.
- **"Do you sell toll passes?"** – No, never. We link to the official operator and add no fee.
- **"Why is my number different?"** – Usually the route (the cheapest road adds time), the car (electric vs petrol) or ferries. Open "Price basis" on the result to see exactly what was used.
- **"What about Sweden / Germany / …?"** – Coverage is strongest in Norway. Other European countries are estimates, and we mark them so.
- **"Is it free?"** – The calculation is free with no account. Trip Pass (79 kr, 14 days) adds GPX export, offline itinerary and navigation links.

**17. Honest fix (builds trust)**
We found our own mistake this week: the fuel-price cards for some countries were out of date and a few showed euro prices labelled as kroner. They now use the same reference prices (EU Weekly Oil Bulletin, 7 Sep 2026) as the calculator, so a country card and a calculated trip can't disagree.
If you spot a number that looks wrong, tell us.

### LinkedIn – "What we got wrong"
Last week a user sent us one screenshot and four words: "the prices here are wrong".
They were right. Our country cards used fixed numbers from a spreadsheet, the trip calculator used a weekly public data source, and nothing forced the two to match. Some Nordic cards even labelled euro prices as kroner.
The fix wasn't to correct the numbers. It was to make the cards read from the same table as the calculator, so they can't drift apart again.
Lesson: when two screens show the same fact, give them one source.
autovere.com
