Tips: har du AutoPASS-brikke, får du rundt 20 % rabatt på de fleste bomstasjoner.

## Short article (EN)

**Driving in Norway: what tolls and ferries really cost**

Norway has no toll booths. Cameras read your number plate and the bill arrives later: through EPASS24 if your car is foreign-registered, or on your AutoPASS account if you have a tag.

A typical trip, Oslo to Bergen (460 km):
- Fastest route: about NOK 413 in tolls.
- Toll-optimised route: about NOK 227, only 14 km longer.
- Toll-free: NOK 0, but over an hour longer.

Fjord ferries are extra. A prepaid AutoPASS ferry agreement can take up to 50% off.

Price your own route: autovere.com

### Svar på kommentarer (kopier ved behov)

- **«Er tallene riktige?»** Tallene er veiledende og bygger på Statens vegvesens bomregister og operatørenes publiserte priser. Vi har kontrollert dem mot en uavhengig kalkulator, og de ligger innenfor ±15 %.
- **«Koster det noe?»** Å beregne turer er gratis. Trip Pass (engangskjøp) låser opp eksport og utskrift i 14 dager.
- **«Is it accurate?»** Figures are indicative, based on official toll registers and published fares, and checked against an independent calculator (within ±15%).
